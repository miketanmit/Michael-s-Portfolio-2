Lenticular Slumping

Research of entropic slumping via air, heat, and gravity, as well as the exploration of not only the object's form but also the lenticular caustics created through the manipulation of light and shadow. Potential directions include (but are not limited to) pavilion ceilings, walls, furniture, and light fixtures.
