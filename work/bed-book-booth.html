Bed & Book Booth - Package Design, Model, & Animation

The 1/8 scale model, package, & animation is part of a larger project completed by VeneerHouse in 2020 for showcase, display, and advertising. Bed & Book Booth is a multi-purpose space for activities like sleeping, studying, and meditating in public and private settings. BBB uses widely distributed standardized 3x6 (910mm x 1820mm) boards arranged in a cubic lattice, which is more versatile than other assembly techniques. 

Summer 2023

Kobayashi Maki Design Workshop x Keio University SFC Campus

Team: Daphne Benedicto, Yutaro Ichinose, Nicholas Keng, Hiroto Kobayashi, Joan Kobayashi, Michael Tan

Created with Rhinoceros 7, Grasshopper, Unreal Engine 5, and Adobe Suite
